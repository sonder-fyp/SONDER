## Provenance

- **Misconception IDs and names:** taken from the Eedi misconception catalogue (`MATH-<n>`). Source/licence record: TODO (confirm licence and redistribution terms before the evaluation).
- **Question authoring:** items and answer options were generated by Claude (LLM) in batches. The misconception-to-option links (the Q-matrix, the `misconceptions` column) were generated the same way.
- **Human review:** Harris reviewed and approved every batch before it was added to the bank. Items enter `bank/items.csv` only after approval. Batches that were not approved (B09) were not added.
- **Automated review (not an approval):** during drafting, independent Claude reviewer agents reviewed batches (up to 3 rounds, `batches/reviews/*_review_round*.txt`) and six Claude auditor agents rated every linked wrong option SOUND / STRETCH / FORCED (`LINK_AUDIT_SUMMARY.md`, `linkaudit_*.txt`, `mapping_audit_*.txt`). These are agent ratings and are **not** human approval.

## Known limitations (do not present as solved)

- **Inseparable misconception pairs:** MATH-1514 / 1773, MATH-1651 / 1074 and MATH-414 / 734 give the same wrong answer on (nearly) every item, so no item separates them.
- **~90 STRETCH links** remain in approved items (plausible but weak option-to-misconception links); see `LINK_AUDIT_SUMMARY.md`.
- **B10:** 6,000 and 600 seconds-per-hour options also fit "100 or 10 seconds in a minute", a belief not in the catalogue.
- **MATH-1773** is never the target of any item, although it appears as a linked option.
- Coverage in `coverage.md` is counted by linked-option appearance, not by `target`.
- B09 is not part of this version.
- **Independent review and validator (Ashher, PR #6):** `python -m validation.math_dataset` reports 0 structural errors, 31 draft-contract findings and 6 topic labels needing an explicit mapping; `--strict-contract` fails by design until the pack decisions are made. See `validation/MATHS_V2_REVIEW.md` (findings M1–M6, each with an owner). Open items that affect this pack:
  - M1: `MATH-1416` links to three options in T6-011B, T6-014B and T6-016B, above the engine draft's limit of two (V11).
  - M2: 6 bank topic labels do not match `topics.csv`, and 26 items link to misconceptions outside their T1–T7 grouping.
  - M3: unlinked and weakly linked T1 rounding options need a decision before T1 is called reviewed.
  - M5: T5-003 wording ("multiplied by itself three times") is ambiguous.
- Per-item source, author/generator, reviewer and date are not columns in `items.csv`; the exact demo subset still needs an explicit provenance and review record before the evaluation freeze.
- No engine import test has been run against this pack yet.
