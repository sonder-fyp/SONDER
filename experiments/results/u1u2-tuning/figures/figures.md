# Figures for u1u2-tuning (tuning)

Generated by `python -m experiments.plot experiments/results/u1u2-tuning` from `metrics.csv`; runs generated at commit `272cdfe445479e21c2aab6c30d832619b50aa833`, configuration SHA-256 `4d1efaa2a9461def62a054aa2ac4c8d6f697c1a8e9bf61c4b57cd0e7531cf199`. Synthetic students under assumed parameters.

**holder-outcomes** — Outcomes for synthetic students who hold one misconception and apply it with the stated probability on triggering items (careful-answer slip 0.08), by unit, apply rate and selection method. Shares of holder runs; 'Wrong label' names a different misconception; 'Missed' ends as none detected; 'Refused' is R3, R4 or R5. Budget 8, default assumed parameters, tuning seeds 1000–1299. Not accuracy on real students.

**holder-rates** — For misconception holders (careful-answer slip 0.08), the wrong-diagnosis rate (left; denominator = runs given any misconception label) and correct coverage (right; denominator = all holder runs), with Wilson 95% intervals, by unit, apply rate and method. Each point pools the unit's misconceptions × 300 seeds; denominators are in metrics.csv. The two panels use separate scales.

**questions-used** — Mean number of questions asked before the session ended, with percentile-bootstrap 95% intervals (2,000 resamples), by unit, truth case and method. Every method has the same budget (8) and the same stopping rules; 300 seeds per case (holders pooled over the unit's misconceptions).

**adverse-labelling** — How often students without the labelled misconception receive one: answer patterns with no named truth (labels are adverse-response labels, not correct or wrong diagnoses) and students with no misconception (false labels), with Wilson 95% intervals. Always-A is deterministic, so under adaptive and fixed selection it is a single run (no interval); under random selection it has 300 runs.
